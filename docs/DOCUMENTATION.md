## Historical evidence

Build batches, validation reports, readiness reviews and verification data live in [`records/`](records/). They are dated evidence of how the product was checked, not current instructions.

[Release notes](RELEASE.md) describe the previously tagged skills release. APP, DESKTOP, CLEAN_INSTALL, OPERATIONAL_INBOX, CONNECTED_CHAT, INTERACTIVE_WORK and earlier batch records describe their dated implementation. Their former screen names, counts, packaging commands and verification results are not current installation instructions.

The [transition](HARNESS_TRANSITION.md) and [retirement record](records/HUB_RETIREMENT_VALIDATION.md) explain what was retained. Historical source documents under `method/source-1.1.0/` remain imported reference material, not authority to activate capabilities. Older reports are retained rather than rewritten to imply they tested today's build.

If guidance conflicts with engine behavior, report the discrepancy. Do not bypass a permission, approval or lock based on Markdown.
