# Company mode

## Owner and sources

The company owner or delegated coordinator validates common identity, priorities and responsibilities. Other contributors can supply examples and specialist knowledge. Technical preparation can be done by the facilitator; do not ask the owner to invent runtime settings.

Review existing company introduction, offers, selected work examples, current operating documents and chosen conversation summaries. Use the actual agreed file plan. If none exists, offer the 19-file baseline in document-map.md. Do not move repositories, reorganize production storage or install a runtime as a side effect.

## Questions — ask only uncovered topics

1. What does the company do, for whom, and what distinguishes its work?
2. Who does what, and which decisions belong to whom?
3. What outcomes matter next, with owner, measure and review date?
4. Which two real workflows would make the first implementation worthwhile?
5. Show one recent assignment: inputs, steps, final output, corrections and time spent.
6. What makes an output acceptable, and who reviews it?
7. Which existing documents are authoritative and already reviewed?
8. Which tools and locations are actually used for the selected work?
9. Who will maintain shared context and methods after onboarding?
10. What remains undecided or deliberately out of scope?

No fabricated metrics: unknown baselines become measurement tasks. A suggested priority remains a suggestion until the owner chooses it.

## Output

Reconcile the shared documents, preserving current approved text and clearly proposing changes. Include sources, ownership, status, effective/review dates where known and open decisions. Draft technical inventories with observed/untested states. A table of candidate tools is not evidence of connections.

Return one common foundation and a short owner review, not separate company foundations for every participant. Explain which member/client information is linked and which company-level decisions should not be restated downstream.
