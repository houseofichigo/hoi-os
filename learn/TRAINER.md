# Facilitator guide

Prepare a separate fictional folder per participant; never connect company accounts. Provide the four fixture files and templates. Use the same task, model, evidence and permissions for baseline/candidate comparisons. Keep answer keys and scoring notes out of the participant assistant session. Do not pre-fill success.

Suggested sequence: modules 1–2 (baseline/inventory), 3–4 (documents/ethics), 5–6 (engine/one workflow), 7–8 (review/handover). Pace depends on participant experience; no validated duration claim. At least three business participants must be observed before teaching validation is claimed.

For each module use its facilitator notes. Observe navigation, missing information, unsupported claims, corrections, human interventions and elapsed time. Exact source matching and factual support are distinct. Meeting agenda is not commercial evidence. Stale synchronization is a freshness limitation, not automatic identity failure. A claim with a real citation can still be unsupported.

Module 7: prepare one separate reviewer session, give only the exact draft and permitted evidence, never private reasoning. Preserve the initial draft, review and corrected version. Resume in a fresh session without transcript history. If no configured host is available, mark the exercise unverified.

Before real delivery: inspect [measurements](MEASUREMENTS.md), independently score factual support, test accessibility and document required course changes. Publication, private adoption and schedule activation are separate decisions.
