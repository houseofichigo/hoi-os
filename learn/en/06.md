## Objective

Specialize one workflow

## Prerequisites

Permitted fictional evidence and an exact selected skill revision.

## Exercise

Choose meeting preparation or marketing positioning. Ask what repeats, what varies, what good output contains and what a human reviews. Discover the installed operations and use permitted retrieval to assemble exact references. Required evidence gaps stop drafting. Prepare and review a private profile using the supported customization flow. Record a cited draft and an evidence checklist; no new context-manifest API is required.

## Participant prompt

> Discover supported operations and select the exact eligible skill revision. Use permitted retrieval; list required evidence, exact references, missing inputs and conflicts before drafting. Label facts, attributed statements, inferences, hypotheses, questions and proposals. An agenda is not evidence of commercial capability; stale calendar synchronization limits freshness, not stable meeting identity.

## Expected output

Evidence checklist and saved cited draft; review boundary explicit.

## Checks

Required inputs resolve or the assistant clarifies. No client guessed from similar titles; private profiles excluded from Git.

## Facilitator notes

Give one team missing offering evidence and another conflicting audience evidence. Neither should silently fill the gap.
