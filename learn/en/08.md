## Objective

Extend and hand over

## Prerequisites

Reviewed pilot results and an identified business owner.

## Exercise

Prepare one department use case, its data boundary, acceptance test, operating owner and recovery plan. Record whether recurring operation is absent, proposed or actually verified. Keep all schedules disabled in the exercise. Hand over the document map, selected workflow, evidence links, limits and human responsibilities. Observe at least three participants before claiming training validation.

## Participant prompt

> Prepare a bounded department harness plan using only reviewed exercise outputs. List useful work, permitted inputs, acceptance checks, responsible reviewer, recovery and explicit non-goals. Describe automation as not enabled unless verified evidence exists. Provide the next manual task and open decisions. Do not install, schedule, publish or adopt into a private workspace.

## Expected output

Department handover and honest evaluation/release status.

## Checks

No invented integrations, schedules, metrics or participant results. Remaining decisions are assigned when known.

## Facilitator notes

Compare initial and final task outputs blind where practical. Record participant difficulties and changes needed.
