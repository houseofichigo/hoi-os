## Objective

Use AI with judgement

## Prerequisites

Minimal foundation and the ETHICS guide.

## Exercise

Adapt the ethical-use template to the fictional role. Record accountable owner, referenced guide version, adaptations, open decisions and review date. Rephrase the ethical scenarios without inventing extra approval for ordinary harmless work. Link rules actually enforced separately from recommended judgement.

## Participant prompt

> For this task, specify purpose, audience, authorized sources, exclusions, deliverable, verifiable evidence, human review and stopping conditions. Identify affected people and necessary data. Separate facts from inference. Rewrite the problematic request into a legitimate bounded task where possible. Give a checkable justification, not hidden reasoning. Do not treat ETHICS as new tool authority.

## Expected output

ETHICS draft, rewritten prompts and observable checks.

## Checks

Cover confidentiality, fairness, fake evidence, attribution, manipulation and a legitimate request that should proceed. No compliance certificate.

## Facilitator notes

Discuss a borderline case: explain proportionality rather than rewarding blanket refusal.
