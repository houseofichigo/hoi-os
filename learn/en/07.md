## Objective

Verify and resume

## Prerequisites

Saved draft, exact revision/hash and supplied evidence; second fresh assistant session.

## Exercise

Check exact citations against supplied evidence and assess factual support separately. Save the draft with a version and checksum. Give a separate reviewer only this draft, permitted evidence and acceptance criteria. Save the report separately; it cannot approve or publish. A corrected draft gets a new version and review. Prepare a short manual handover with links to records, known state, pending decisions and next action; use it in a fresh session. This exercise does not require an automated resume operation.

## Participant prompt

> Read the selected draft and resolve its exact evidence with current permissions. Report unsupported claims, gaps and required human decisions. Do not infer approval from a review. Prepare a bounded handover identifying the version/checksum, objective, known outcome, review status and recorded next action; unknown stays unknown. In a fresh session, recheck the records before continuing.

## Expected output

Structural results, assisted report, human review sheet and fresh-session handover.

## Checks

A valid citation with unsupported claim is caught by review; a changed draft never inherits an old report. No whole prior conversation is pasted.

## Facilitator notes

Separate four judgements: execution, exact references, factual support and human approval. Measure corrections/time; do not infer a gain.
