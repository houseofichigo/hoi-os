# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Discuss the work and its evidence;
avoid harassment, discrimination, threats and publishing another person's private
information. Use fictional examples and redact sensitive data in reports.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions and restrict participation.
For conduct concerns, request a private contact from maintainers without posting
sensitive details publicly. A dedicated private reporting channel and response
commitment are not yet established for this alpha.
